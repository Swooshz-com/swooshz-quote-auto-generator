Internal UAT Terms

Swooshz Quote Generator Terms of Use

Version: Internal UAT 0.1
Effective date: 15 July 2026

These interim terms describe the permitted internal testing use of the Swooshz Quote Generator. Final legal review and organisation details remain required before production use.

Permitted Use

Use is limited to authorised internal evaluation and quotation workflow testing. Do not use this UAT system for public access, live customer delivery, payment processing, or production commitments.

User Responsibilities

Users must review quotation inputs, pricing matches, totals, and exported workbooks before relying on them. Do not upload secrets, payment data, or unnecessary personal data.

Generated Output

Generated quotation output is a working draft for authorised review. The responsible user must confirm the quote basis and resolve missing or unclear pricing before issuing any quotation.

Availability And Changes

This internal UAT service may be changed, suspended, or withdrawn during testing. No production availability commitment is made by this page.

Retention And Evidence

Generation evidence, audit events, quote artifacts, sessions, and feedback are designed to be retained for three calendar years, subject to approved legal holds and deletion controls. Production log retention is designed for 90 days; local synthetic UAT logs may remain for up to 30 days.
