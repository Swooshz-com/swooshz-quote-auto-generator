Swooshz Quote Generator Privacy Notice

Version: Internal UAT 0.1
Effective date: 15 July 2026

This notice explains how personal data may be collected, used, disclosed, stored, and protected when you use the Swooshz Quote Generator.

Applicable requirements may include Singapore's PDPA, the GDPR, or other privacy laws; the responsible organisation and legal basis must be confirmed before production use.

Personal Data We May Collect

We may collect information you provide when preparing quotations, including names, job titles, company names, addresses, project details, uploaded reference files, quotation content, and related communications.

How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data to prepare quotations, manage customer and project records, provide requested services, respond to enquiries, improve our workflow, maintain security, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

Disclosure Of Personal Data

We may disclose personal data to service providers, contractors, professional advisers, authorities, or other parties where necessary for quotation preparation, service delivery, business operations, legal compliance, or protection of rights.

Cross-Border Transfers

Any cross-border transfer controls, destinations, and legal mechanisms must be confirmed by the responsible organisation before production use. This internal UAT page does not claim that a particular transfer mechanism has been implemented.

Retention

Generation evidence, audit events, quote artifacts, sessions, and feedback are designed to be retained for three calendar years. Production routine logs are designed for 90 days, while local synthetic UAT logs may remain for up to 30 days. Approved legal holds pause deletion while preserving the original expiry date.

Feedback And Diagnostic Linking

When you open the feedback form, SQAG proposes only the current authorised generation run and quote session pair, or the current quote session alone, when that context can be matched safely in your workspace. Before submission, the form shows the proposed context and the privacy-minimised technical metadata involved. You may remove the automatic link, submit without quote context, or add a manual reference. Full quotation evidence remains in the protected forensic store and is not copied into routine logs or the feedback report. Necessary support access to linked evidence must be authorised and audited.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, disposal, or similar risks.

Your Rights

Available rights and the process for exercising them depend on the applicable law and the responsible organisation. Those details require legal confirmation before production use.
